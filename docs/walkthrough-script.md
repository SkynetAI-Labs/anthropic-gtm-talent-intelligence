# Suggested 2–3 minute walkthrough

## 0:00–0:25 — Start here

This independent case study shows how I would bring recruitment experience and commercial judgment to EMEA GTM hiring. I start with role calibration, then map talent and investigate evidence gaps. All records and results are fictional.

## 0:25–0:50 — Part 1

The research separates public vacancy evidence from sourcing hypotheses. London, Paris, Munich and Dublin examples frame the search. Adjacent cloud, software, security and consulting experience needs careful assessment rather than assumptions based on employer names.

## 0:50–1:15 — Part 2

Here are 50 mapped prospects. Thirty have entered an application process. I can filter the pool and inspect evidence. Mapping someone does not make them an applicant.

## 1:15–1:50 — Part 3

The opening candidate has gaps. The score explains which evidence contributes, while screening questions help investigate what is missing. I would challenge the examples and consider equivalent experience. Human review controls progression.

## 1:50–2:15 — Part 4

Five simulated searches have six offers and four acceptances. One offer was declined and one remains pending. I distinguish those outcomes instead of treating the raw ratio as a final close rate.

## 2:15–2:45 — Part 5

My first 90 days would begin with learning the business and agreeing the hiring bar. I would validate sourcing, support assessment and refine the approach using pipeline evidence and candidate feedback. This demonstrates my approach, not delivered results for Anthropic.
