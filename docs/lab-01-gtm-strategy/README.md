# Part 1 — GTM recruitment strategy

### Role profiles

| Role | Evidence I would look for |
|---|---|
| Enterprise Account Executive | Complex deals, discovery and negotiation |
| Business Development Representative | Targeted prospecting and qualification |
| Sales Engineer | Technical discovery, solution design and evaluation |
| Account Manager | Adoption, account planning and stakeholder relationships |
| Sales Leadership | Coaching, forecasting and resource decisions |

### EMEA market focus

| Market | Search focus informed by public role examples |
|---|---|
| London | Enterprise and sector sales, technical advisory |
| Dublin | Startup sales and sales development |
| Paris | Financial services and automotive accounts |
| Munich / DACH | Customer adoption and regional account experience |

### My seven-step operating approach

**1 Intake** — agree outcomes. **2 Calibrate** — define credible evidence. **3 Map** — compare direct and adjacent talent. **4 Engage** — communicate the opportunity. **5 Assess** — investigate contribution and gaps. **6 Close** — understand motivations early. **7 Learn** — review pipeline evidence and candidate feedback.
