# Recruitment calibration record — rules 1.1

This is a desk review of illustrative rules, not validation by Anthropic or hiring experts.

Each scorecard prioritises role-specific evidence: enterprise deal work for account executives; prospecting and qualification for BDRs; discovery and solution design for sales engineers; adoption and planning for account managers; coaching and forecasting for leadership. Each role includes three substantive interview questions, supplemented by gap-specific prompts.

Weights remain 75 points for competency evidence, 15 for relevant experience and 10 for industry context. These are illustrative choices. Review equivalent capability and adjacent-sector experience. The default candidate has gaps to demonstrate screening judgment; ranking and scores remain unchanged.

A score of 100 indicates complete configured evidence fields and benchmark coverage. It does not prove perfect ability or evidence truth. Synthetic notes illustrate scenarios, not audited outcomes. Missing evidence invites human investigation rather than automatic rejection.

Before operational use, calibrate actual requirements with the hiring manager and a technical partner. Compare direct and adjacent profiles, inspect disagreements and revise the scorecard. Independently evaluate job relevance, fairness and evidence quality. This portfolio does not claim those activities have occurred.
