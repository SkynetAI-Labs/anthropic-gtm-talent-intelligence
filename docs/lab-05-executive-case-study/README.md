# Part 5 — Executive recruitment case study

## My approach

I bring 20+ years of full-cycle recruitment, executive search, international hiring and business development. My experience includes recruiting sales managers, sales engineers and business development professionals for industrial and energy clients, including Siemens and Dresser Rand.

My historical delivery includes an approximately 75% fill rate, typical time-to-fill of 1–2 weeks, and around 170 personnel mobilised across two international power projects. At Ascent Energy Global, I grew the business from one to 11 employees, with approximately US$100,000 in monthly contractor revenue. Those results inform how I scope a search, qualify requirements and work with candidates through to acceptance.

## Applying that experience to GTM hiring

I would begin with the customer's commercial problem and the outcomes the new hire needs to deliver. I would agree essential competencies with the hiring manager, map direct and adjacent talent, and use structured questions to test personal contribution. For technical-commercial roles, I would work with a technical partner to assess discovery, integration and evaluation skills.

The application connects that approach to a visible workflow: map prospects, examine evidence, record recruiter judgment and review pipeline progress. Its simulated portfolio keeps mapped prospects separate from applicants and pending offers separate from final outcomes.

## My first 90 days

| Period | Priorities | Evidence of progress |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Learn the business, agree search priorities and calibrate role scorecards | Agreed briefs, evidence standards and a baseline pipeline |
| Days 31–60 | Validate market maps, test outreach and run structured screens | Relevant conversations, useful candidate evidence and manager feedback |
| Days 61–90 | Refine sourcing allocation, support closing and agree next-quarter priorities | Search progress, offer insight and documented process improvements |

## What I would bring to the conversation

I combine international recruitment experience with commercial development and practical AI-assisted tooling. I would use the tool to make evidence and uncertainty easier to discuss, while retaining responsibility for candidate experience and recruiting decisions.
